Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

UtilsWeb (“we”, “us”) operates the website utilsweb.com, a free collection of online utility tools. This Privacy Policy explains what information is and is not collected when you use our tools, and how third‑party advertising works on this site. We have designed UtilsWeb to be private by default.

1. Files and data you process never leave your browser

Every tool on UtilsWeb runs 100% client‑side in your own browser using JavaScript and, where needed, WebAssembly. Any text, images, documents, or other files you process with our tools are handled entirely on your device. We never upload, store, transmit, or have access to the content you process. There is no server that receives your files. Once you close or reload the page, that data is gone.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not ask you to create an account, we do not require any sign‑up, and we do not collect names, email addresses, or the contents of your work. We do not sell or share personal information, because we do not collect it in the first place.

3. Cookies and advertising (Google AdSense)

To keep UtilsWeb free, we display ads served by Google AdSense. Third‑party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to UtilsWeb and/or other sites on the Internet.

  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can learn how Google uses data when you use our partners’ sites at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • For more on third‑party ad cookies, visit aboutads.info/choices or youronlinechoices.eu.

4. Analytics

We keep analytics to a strict minimum. If any privacy‑respecting, aggregate analytics are used, they do not identify you individually and contain none of the content you process in the tools.

5. Children’s privacy

UtilsWeb is a general‑audience site and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “last updated” date at the top reflects the latest revision. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at sidkfree@gmail.com or see our Contact page.
