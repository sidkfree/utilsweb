Color Picker from Image

Drop a photo, click anywhere to get the exact HEX, RGB and HSL. Auto-extracts the dominant 8-color palette.

How it works

Your image is loaded into a hidden <canvas>. Each click reads the exact pixel under the cursor via getImageData() — no server round-trip, no upload. The palette is extracted by quantizing all the pixels into the most common color buckets (a quick median-cut-style grouping over a downsampled copy of the image, runs in < 100 ms on a typical photo).

Use cases: matching brand colors from a screenshot, picking accent colors from a moodboard photo, sampling Pantone-like swatches from product shots, copying HEX values out of a logo without opening Photoshop.

🔒 100% client-side — nothing ever leaves your browser.
