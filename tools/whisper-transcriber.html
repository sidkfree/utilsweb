How it works

OpenAI's Whisper model is loaded into your browser via transformers.js + ONNX Runtime Web. The model files (~75 MB for tiny.en) are downloaded the first time and then cached in your browser's storage — subsequent transcriptions are instant. Your audio never leaves the page.

Model picker: Tiny is fast and good for clear English. Base is slower but more accurate. Multilingual models support 90+ languages — pick the language hint above. WebGPU acceleration is used automatically if your browser/GPU supports it.

Privacy: commercial transcription APIs cost $0.006–$0.024/minute and upload your audio. This tool is free, offline-capable after first load, and the audio never touches a server.

About Whisper Transcriber

Whisper Transcriber is a free online tool that lets you speak into your mic or drop an audio file → get a text transcript. <strong>Whisper-tiny running locally in your browser.</strong>. It belongs to our Utility collection and, like every UtilsWeb tool, it runs entirely inside your web browser — there is no upload, no sign‑up, and nothing ever leaves your device. That makes it fast, private, and free to use as often as you like.

How to use Whisper Transcriber

  1. Open Whisper Transcriber — it loads instantly, with no account or download needed.
  2. Enter or load your input directly in the tool above.
  3. Get your result in real time; everything is computed locally in your browser.
  4. Copy, download, or reuse the output — and reload the page anytime to start fresh.

Frequently asked questions

Is Whisper Transcriber free to use?

Yes. Whisper Transcriber is completely free, with no sign‑up, no account, and no usage limits. Like every tool on UtilsWeb, it is supported by unobtrusive ads so it can stay free for everyone.

Does Whisper Transcriber upload my data to a server?

No. Whisper Transcriber runs entirely in your browser. Anything you enter or load is processed on your own device and is never uploaded, stored, or sent to us. When you close the page, the data is gone.

Do I need to install anything to use Whisper Transcriber?

No installation is required. Whisper Transcriber works directly in any modern web browser on desktop or mobile. Because it is client‑side, it even keeps working if you lose your internet connection after the page has loaded.

Is Whisper Transcriber accurate and safe to use?

Whisper Transcriber uses well‑established methods and runs locally, so there is no risk of your information leaking to a third party. For important financial, medical, or legal decisions we still recommend verifying results independently.
