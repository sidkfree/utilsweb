How it works

OpenAI's Whisper model is loaded into your browser via transformers.js + ONNX Runtime Web. The model files (~75 MB for tiny.en) are downloaded the first time and then cached in your browser's storage — subsequent transcriptions are instant. Your audio never leaves the page.

Model picker: Tiny is fast and good for clear English. Base is slower but more accurate. Multilingual models support 90+ languages — pick the language hint above. WebGPU acceleration is used automatically if your browser/GPU supports it.

Privacy: commercial transcription APIs cost $0.006–$0.024/minute and upload your audio. This tool is free, offline-capable after first load, and the audio never touches a server.

🔒 100% client-side — nothing ever leaves your browser.
