MP4 → MP3

Extract the audio track from a video file (mp4, webm, mov, mkv).

⬇ Download MP3

How it works

Uses ffmpeg.wasm — a WebAssembly build of FFmpeg that runs in your browser. First load is ~25 MB; subsequent runs are fast.

🔒 100% client-side — nothing ever leaves your browser.
