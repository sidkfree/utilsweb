ERC-20 Unit Converter

Convert between raw integer units and human-readable amounts for any ERC-20 token.

How it works

raw = human × 10^decimals. USDC uses 6, most tokens use 18. BigInt math = no precision loss.

🔒 100% client-side — nothing ever leaves your browser.
