Base64 Encoder / Decoder

Encode or decode text and files to/from Base64.

How it works

Uses the browser's native btoa / atob with UTF-8 safety wrappers. Files are read with FileReader and never leave your device.

🔒 100% client-side — nothing ever leaves your browser.
