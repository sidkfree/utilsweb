Focal Length Equivalent

Convert a lens's focal length between sensor formats (FF, APS-C, M4/3…).

How it works

Equivalent focal = actual × crop factor. The result is what the field-of-view would look like on the target sensor.

🔒 100% client-side — nothing ever leaves your browser.
