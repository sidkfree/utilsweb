How it works

An ONNX (Open Neural Network Exchange) model from @imgly/background-removal runs through onnxruntime-web directly in your tab. The first load downloads the model (~30 MB) and caches it in your browser — subsequent uses are instant. Your photo never leaves the page.

Works well on: people, products on a clean background, animals, objects. Struggles with: hair detail, motion-blurred subjects, low-contrast cases where subject and background share the same color.

Privacy upside: remove.bg and competitors upload your photo. We don't — that's a meaningful difference for ID photos, medical shots, or anything sensitive.

🔒 100% client-side — nothing ever leaves your browser.
