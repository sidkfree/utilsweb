How it works

The image is fed to a TensorFlow.js ESRGAN super-resolution model via UpscalerJS. The model runs entirely on your device using WebGL (or WebGPU when available). On a typical laptop, a 500×500 image takes 3–10 seconds at 2×.

Best results: small but in-focus photos. Logos, faces, product shots, screenshots of old/low-res images.

Less good on: already-blurry sources (AI can't invent detail that isn't there), heavily-compressed JPEGs (the artifacts get amplified).
