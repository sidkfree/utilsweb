QR Code Generator

Type anything — URL, Wi-Fi password, plain text. Get a QR code you can save as PNG.

⬇ Download PNG

How it works

This page runs entirely in your browser using the qrcodejs library. Nothing is sent to any server — you can disconnect from the internet after the page loads and it still works. The PNG download is generated locally from the canvas.

Wi-Fi tip: to generate a QR that auto-joins Wi-Fi, paste WIFI:T:WPA;S:NetworkName;P:Password;; in the content field.

🔒 100% client-side — nothing ever leaves your browser.
