Credit Card Validator

Luhn check + card brand detection (Visa, MC, Amex, Discover, JCB, Diners…).

How it works

The Luhn (mod-10) algorithm catches single-digit typos and most transpositions. Brand detection uses BIN-range regexes.

🔒 100% client-side — nothing ever leaves your browser.
