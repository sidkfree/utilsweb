Ethereum Address Validator

Check syntax + EIP-55 checksum of an Ethereum address.

How it works

Checks the 0x + 40-hex shape. Full EIP-55 checksum verification needs keccak256 which the browser doesn't ship natively — paste into MetaMask for that final check.

🔒 100% client-side — nothing ever leaves your browser.
