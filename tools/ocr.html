How it works

Your image is processed by Tesseract.js — a pure-JavaScript port of Google's Tesseract OCR engine. The language model (~5–15 MB depending on language) is downloaded the first time and cached. Your image never leaves the page.

Best results on: typed text, screenshots, scans, business cards, signs. Tougher on: handwriting, low contrast, rotated text — try rotating the image first if accuracy is poor.

Privacy: commercial OCR APIs (Google Vision, AWS Textract) cost $1–$5 per 1000 pages and upload your scans. This is free + private — useful for receipts, ID cards, medical scans, contracts.

🔒 100% client-side — nothing ever leaves your browser.
