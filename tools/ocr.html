How it works

Your image is processed by Tesseract.js — a pure-JavaScript port of Google's Tesseract OCR engine. The language model (~5–15 MB depending on language) is downloaded the first time and cached. Your image never leaves the page.

Best results on: typed text, screenshots, scans, business cards, signs. Tougher on: handwriting, low contrast, rotated text — try rotating the image first if accuracy is poor.

Privacy: commercial OCR APIs (Google Vision, AWS Textract) cost $1–$5 per 1000 pages and upload your scans. This is free + private — useful for receipts, ID cards, medical scans, contracts.

About OCR — Photo to Text

OCR — Photo to Text is a free online tool that lets you drop a screenshot, scan, business card or photo of handwriting → clean text. 100+ languages. It belongs to our Utility collection and, like every UtilsWeb tool, it runs entirely inside your web browser — there is no upload, no sign‑up, and nothing ever leaves your device. That makes it fast, private, and free to use as often as you like.

Frequently asked questions

Yes. OCR — Photo to Text is completely free, with no sign‑up, no account, and no usage limits. Like every tool on UtilsWeb, it is supported by unobtrusive ads so it can stay free for everyone.

No. OCR — Photo to Text runs entirely in your browser. Anything you enter or load is processed on your own device and is never uploaded, stored, or sent to us. When you close the page, the data is gone.

Do I need to install anything to use OCR — Photo to Text?

No installation is required. OCR — Photo to Text works directly in any modern web browser on desktop or mobile. Because it is client‑side, it even keeps working if you lose your internet connection after the page has loaded.
