MAC Address Generator

Generate random MAC addresses — locally administered or per OUI.

How it works

Crypto-random bytes. LAA mode sets bit-1 of the first byte (the U/L bit) per IEEE 802 — safe for VMs and virtual NICs.

🔒 100% client-side — nothing ever leaves your browser.
