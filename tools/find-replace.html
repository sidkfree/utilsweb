Find & Replace

Bulk find-and-replace with optional regex and case-insensitive matching.

How it works

Uses native JavaScript String.replace + RegExp — runs entirely in your browser. Special regex characters are auto-escaped unless you tick Regex.

🔒 100% client-side — nothing ever leaves your browser.
