How it works

Your .docx is unpacked in your browser with mammoth.js (it understands the Word XML format) and rendered to clean HTML. The HTML is then opened in the browser's native print pipeline — pick Save as PDF in the print dialog to download. This avoids the canvas-rasterization limits that break very long documents (40+ pages), and the resulting PDF has selectable, searchable text.

Scope: Headings, paragraphs, bold/italic, bullet/numbered lists, tables, hyperlinks and images embedded in the docx are preserved. Complex Word features (multi-column layouts, text boxes, watermarks, embedded fonts, smart art) may degrade. For pixel-perfect Office output, use Word's own Save as PDF.
