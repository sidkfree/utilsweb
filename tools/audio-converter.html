Audio Converter

Convert between MP3, WAV, OGG, M4A, FLAC.

⬇ Download

How it works

FFmpeg (WebAssembly) reads any browser-supported audio container and re-encodes to your target codec. WAV / FLAC are lossless; the bitrate slider only applies to lossy formats.

🔒 100% client-side — nothing ever leaves your browser.
