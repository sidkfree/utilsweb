AES Encryption

AES-GCM 256-bit encrypt / decrypt for text. Browser-native crypto.

How it works

PBKDF2-SHA256 (200k iterations) derives a 256-bit AES-GCM key from your password. Output = base64(salt | iv | ciphertext). Browser-native WebCrypto — no library, no upload.

🔒 100% client-side — nothing ever leaves your browser.
