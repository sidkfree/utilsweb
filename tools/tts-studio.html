How it works

Meta's MMS-TTS model (Massively Multilingual Speech, 1100+ languages) is loaded into your tab via transformers.js + ONNX Runtime Web. The text is tokenised, run through the model on your CPU/GPU, and decoded into a 16 kHz WAV that you can play and download. Your text never leaves the page.

Use cases: voice-over for videos, audiobook drafts, accessibility, learning pronunciation. Limits: single fixed voice per language (no voice cloning here); long paragraphs are batched and concatenated.

Privacy: ElevenLabs / OpenAI TTS / Google charge per character and upload your text. This is free and your text/audio stays local.

About Text-to-Speech Studio

Text-to-Speech Studio is a free online tool that lets you type any text, click Generate, hear it spoken in 60+ languages — all locally in your browser. It belongs to our Utility collection and, like every UtilsWeb tool, it runs entirely inside your web browser — there is no upload, no sign‑up, and nothing ever leaves your device. That makes it fast, private, and free to use as often as you like.

How to use Text-to-Speech Studio

  1. Open Text-to-Speech Studio — it loads instantly, with no account or download needed.
  2. Enter or load your input directly in the tool above.
  3. Get your result in real time; everything is computed locally in your browser.
  4. Copy, download, or reuse the output — and reload the page anytime to start fresh.

Frequently asked questions

Is Text-to-Speech Studio free to use?

Yes. Text-to-Speech Studio is completely free, with no sign‑up, no account, and no usage limits. Like every tool on UtilsWeb, it is supported by unobtrusive ads so it can stay free for everyone.

Does Text-to-Speech Studio upload my data to a server?

No. Text-to-Speech Studio runs entirely in your browser. Anything you enter or load is processed on your own device and is never uploaded, stored, or sent to us. When you close the page, the data is gone.

Do I need to install anything to use Text-to-Speech Studio?

No installation is required. Text-to-Speech Studio works directly in any modern web browser on desktop or mobile. Because it is client‑side, it even keeps working if you lose your internet connection after the page has loaded.

Is Text-to-Speech Studio accurate and safe to use?

Text-to-Speech Studio uses well‑established methods and runs locally, so there is no risk of your information leaking to a third party. For important financial, medical, or legal decisions we still recommend verifying results independently.
