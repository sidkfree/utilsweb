Text Diff

Compare two blocks of text side-by-side with line-level highlighting.

How it works

A standard longest-common-subsequence (LCS) diff runs locally in your browser. Lines unique to the original appear in red; lines unique to the changed version appear in green.

About Text Diff

Text Diff is a free online tool that lets you compare two blocks of text side-by-side with line-level highlighting. It belongs to our Text & Markdown collection and, like every UtilsWeb tool, it runs entirely inside your web browser — there is no upload, no sign‑up, and nothing ever leaves your device. That makes it fast, private, and free to use as often as you like.

How to use Text Diff

  1. Open Text Diff — it loads instantly, with no account or download needed.
  2. Enter or load your input directly in the tool above.
  3. Get your result in real time; everything is computed locally in your browser.
  4. Copy, download, or reuse the output — and reload the page anytime to start fresh.

Frequently asked questions

Is Text Diff free to use?

Yes. Text Diff is completely free, with no sign‑up, no account, and no usage limits. Like every tool on UtilsWeb, it is supported by unobtrusive ads so it can stay free for everyone.

Does Text Diff upload my data to a server?

No. Text Diff runs entirely in your browser. Anything you enter or load is processed on your own device and is never uploaded, stored, or sent to us. When you close the page, the data is gone.

Do I need to install anything to use Text Diff?

No installation is required. Text Diff works directly in any modern web browser on desktop or mobile. Because it is client‑side, it even keeps working if you lose your internet connection after the page has loaded.

Is Text Diff accurate and safe to use?

Text Diff uses well‑established methods and runs locally, so there is no risk of your information leaking to a third party. For important financial, medical, or legal decisions we still recommend verifying results independently.

Your privacy matters: Text Diff processes everything on your device. Read our Privacy Policy.
