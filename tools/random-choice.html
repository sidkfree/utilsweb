Random Choice / Picker

Paste a list, pick N at random — perfect for giveaways and decisions.

How it works

Without-replacement uses Fisher-Yates shuffle. With-replacement uses one crypto-random index per draw.

🔒 100% client-side — nothing ever leaves your browser.
