Hash Generator

SHA-1, SHA-256, SHA-384, SHA-512 — for text or files.

How it works

Uses the browser's built-in crypto.subtle.digest (WebCrypto API). Note: MD5 is intentionally excluded — it is broken for security. SHA-256+ is the modern default.

🔒 100% client-side — nothing ever leaves your browser.
