How it works

Max HR ≈ 220 − age. With a resting HR, switches to the Karvonen formula: target = rest + (max − rest) × intensity.

About Heart Rate Zones

Heart Rate Zones is a free online tool that lets you training zones from your age (and optional resting HR via Karvonen). It belongs to our Health & Fitness collection and, like every UtilsWeb tool, it runs entirely inside your web browser — there is no upload, no sign‑up, and nothing ever leaves your device. That makes it fast, private, and free to use as often as you like.

How to use Heart Rate Zones

  1. Open Heart Rate Zones — it loads instantly, with no account or download needed.
  2. Enter or load your input directly in the tool above.
  3. Get your result in real time; everything is computed locally in your browser.
  4. Copy, download, or reuse the output — and reload the page anytime to start fresh.

Frequently asked questions

Is Heart Rate Zones free to use?

Yes. Heart Rate Zones is completely free, with no sign‑up, no account, and no usage limits. Like every tool on UtilsWeb, it is supported by unobtrusive ads so it can stay free for everyone.

Does Heart Rate Zones upload my data to a server?

No. Heart Rate Zones runs entirely in your browser. Anything you enter or load is processed on your own device and is never uploaded, stored, or sent to us. When you close the page, the data is gone.

Do I need to install anything to use Heart Rate Zones?

No installation is required. Heart Rate Zones works directly in any modern web browser on desktop or mobile. Because it is client‑side, it even keeps working if you lose your internet connection after the page has loaded.

Is Heart Rate Zones accurate and safe to use?

Heart Rate Zones uses well‑established methods and runs locally, so there is no risk of your information leaking to a third party. For important financial, medical, or legal decisions we still recommend verifying results independently.
