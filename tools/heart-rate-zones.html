Heart Rate Zones

Training zones from your age (and optional resting HR via Karvonen).

Max HR: bpm

How it works

Max HR ≈ 220 − age. With a resting HR, switches to the Karvonen formula: target = rest + (max − rest) × intensity.

🔒 100% client-side — nothing ever leaves your browser.
