Frequently asked questions

  
Is Regex Tester free to use?
Yes. Regex Tester is completely free, with no sign‑up, no account, and no usage limits. Like every tool on UtilsWeb, it is supported by unobtrusive ads so it can stay free for everyone.
Does Regex Tester upload my data to a server?
No. Regex Tester runs entirely in your browser. Anything you enter or load is processed on your own device and is never uploaded, stored, or sent to us. When you close the page, the data is gone.
Do I need to install anything to use Regex Tester?
No installation is required. Regex Tester works directly in any modern web browser on desktop or mobile. Because it is client‑side, it even keeps working if you lose your internet connection after the page has loaded.
Is Regex Tester accurate and safe to use?
Regex Tester uses well‑established methods and runs locally, so there is no risk of your information leaking to a third party. For important financial, medical, or legal decisions we still recommend verifying results independently.
