How it works

Your photo is parsed in your browser with exifr — a tiny pure-JavaScript EXIF parser. Every metadata block (EXIF, IPTC, XMP, GPS) is read and listed. The "Strip" button redraws the pixels into a fresh <canvas> and re-encodes them with toBlob() — re-encoding always discards metadata, so the output has zero EXIF leak.

Why this matters: photos taken on your phone carry your exact GPS coordinates, device serial, software version, and timestamp. Posting them to any site (Twitter, Instagram, blog) without stripping is a privacy leak. Most platforms strip on upload, but plenty of small forums, image hosts, and email do not.

About EXIF Viewer & Stripper

EXIF Viewer & Stripper is a free online tool that lets you see every hidden metadata field in your photo — camera, GPS, timestamp — and strip them with one click.
