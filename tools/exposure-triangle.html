Exposure Triangle Calculator

Change one of ISO / aperture / shutter — keep equivalent exposure.

EV @ ISO 100

How it works

EV = log₂(N²/t) − log₂(ISO/100). Any (ISO, N, t) trio with the same EV gives the same brightness.
