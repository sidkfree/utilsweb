About Typing Speed Test

Typing Speed Test is a free online tool that lets you measure your words-per-minute and accuracy on a 1-minute prompt. It belongs to our Utility collection and, like every UtilsWeb tool, it runs entirely inside your web browser — there is no upload, no sign‑up, and nothing ever leaves your device. That makes it fast, private, and free to use as often as you like.

How to use Typing Speed Test

  1. Open Typing Speed Test — it loads instantly, with no account or download needed.

  3. Get your result in real time; everything is computed locally in your browser.
  4. Copy, download, or reuse the output — and reload the page anytime to start fresh.

Frequently asked questions

Is Typing Speed Test free to use?

Yes. Typing Speed Test is completely free, with no sign‑up, no account, and no usage limits. Like every tool on UtilsWeb, it is supported by unobtrusive ads so it can stay free for everyone.

Does Typing Speed Test upload my data to a server?

No. Typing Speed Test runs entirely in your browser. Anything you enter or load is processed on your own device and is never uploaded, stored, or sent to us. When you close the page, the data is gone.

Do I need to install anything to use Typing Speed Test?

No installation is required. Typing Speed Test works directly in any modern web browser on desktop or mobile. Because it is client‑side, it even keeps working if you lose your internet connection after the page has loaded.

Is Typing Speed Test accurate and safe to use?

Typing Speed Test uses well‑established methods and runs locally, so there is no risk of your information leaking to a third party. For important financial, medical, or legal decisions we still recommend verifying results independently.
