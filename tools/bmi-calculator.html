BMI Calculator

Body Mass Index — metric or imperial — with WHO category.

How it works

BMI = weight (kg) / height² (m). Categories follow WHO thresholds. BMI ignores muscle mass — use it as one signal among many.

🔒 100% client-side — nothing ever leaves your browser.
