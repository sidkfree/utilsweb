My Public IP

Show your public IPv4 / IPv6 and (rough) geolocation.

How it works

Uses public free APIs (ipify, ipapi.co) — they see your IP from your direct connection. Open-source alternatives include ipinfo.io. This is the only tool here that contacts external servers (it has to, to learn your public IP).

🔒 100% client-side — nothing ever leaves your browser.
