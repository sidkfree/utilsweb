PDF Splitter

Extract specific pages or page ranges from a PDF into a new file.

⬇ Download PDF

How it works

Page ranges are parsed (1,3-5 → pages 1, 3, 4, 5), then pdf-lib copies those pages into a fresh PDF. Your file never leaves your browser.

🔒 100% client-side — nothing ever leaves your browser.
