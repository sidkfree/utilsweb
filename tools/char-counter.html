Length Checker

Check character limits for Twitter, SMS, SEO titles, meta descriptions.

How it works

Lengths are computed live in your browser. Numbers reflect publicly documented limits — emoji count as 2 on most platforms; this tool counts every Unicode code unit equally.

🔒 100% client-side — nothing ever leaves your browser.
