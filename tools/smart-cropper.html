How it works

This page loads MediaPipe Face Detection in your browser via WebAssembly. When you drop a photo, the model finds the face (or main subject) on your device — no server, no upload — and centers the crop window around it. The canvas then renders the chosen aspect ratio and offers an instant JPG/PNG download. The file never leaves your machine.

Group photos: the crop is centered on the centroid of all detected faces, so everyone stays in frame.

No face? The tool falls back to a smart centered crop and lets you nudge it with arrows.

🔒 100% client-side — nothing ever leaves your browser.
