PDF Compressor

Shrink a PDF by re-encoding embedded images at lower quality.

⬇ Download PDF

How it works

Each page is rendered with pdf.js, re-encoded as JPEG at your chosen quality, then embedded into a fresh PDF with pdf-lib. Text becomes raster — searchability is lost, but file size drops dramatically.

🔒 100% client-side — nothing ever leaves your browser.
