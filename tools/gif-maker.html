How it works

The video is decoded by your browser's native <video> element. We seek to each frame in the trim window, draw it onto a hidden <canvas>, and feed the pixel data into gif.js (a pure-JavaScript GIF encoder that runs in a Web Worker so the page stays responsive).

Tips: shorter clips + smaller widths + fewer FPS = much smaller files. A 3-second 480 px @ 10 fps clip is typically 1–3 MB; full-HD GIFs balloon quickly past 20 MB.

🔒 100% client-side — nothing ever leaves your browser.
