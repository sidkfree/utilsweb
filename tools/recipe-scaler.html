Recipe Scaler

Scale a recipe up or down by serving count, preserving every ingredient ratio.

How it works

Multiplies the leading quantity on each line by (desired/original). Recognises fractions (1/2, 1 1/4) and commas.

🔒 100% client-side — nothing ever leaves your browser.
