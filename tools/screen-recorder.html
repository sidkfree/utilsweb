How it works

The browser's getDisplayMedia() API asks the OS to capture a screen, window or tab. That live stream is piped into MediaRecorder, which encodes WebM (VP9 or VP8 depending on browser) directly into a blob in memory. Nothing is uploaded — the file is yours the moment recording stops.

Audio: "system audio" is only available when you pick a Chrome tab source (and tick "Share tab audio" in the picker). When you pick a window or full screen, system audio is silently dropped on most OSes — that's a platform limitation, not a bug. Mic audio works in all modes.

Convert to MP4? WebM is the native browser output. To convert, drop the result into our future MP4 converter tool, or just keep the .webm — every modern player handles it.
