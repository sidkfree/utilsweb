About UtilsWeb

Tools that just work — privately, in your browser.

UtilsWeb is a free, growing collection of over 150 online utility tools covering finance, health, SEO, development, design, text, images, documents, crypto, cooking and everyday life. Our goal is simple: give you fast, reliable, no‑nonsense tools that respect your privacy and your time.

What makes UtilsWeb different

  • 100% client‑side. Every calculation and conversion runs in your browser. Your data never touches a server — there is nothing to upload and nothing for us to store.
  • No sign‑up, no paywall. Open a tool and use it immediately. Every tool is free.
  • Fast and lightweight. Pages load instantly and most tools work even after you go offline.
  • Genuinely useful. Each tool solves one real problem well, with a clear explanation of how it works.

How it works

Modern browsers are powerful. Using JavaScript and WebAssembly, UtilsWeb performs tasks — from compressing an image to formatting JSON or calculating a loan — right on your device. This “local‑first” approach means better privacy, lower latency, and zero bandwidth cost for your files.

Who builds it

UtilsWeb is built and maintained by an independent developer who believes simple tools shouldn’t require accounts, ads that follow you everywhere, or uploading your private files to a stranger’s server. New tools are added regularly. Have a suggestion? Get in touch.
